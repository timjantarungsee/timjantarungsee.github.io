Considered by some sensationalist sources to be "The last hunter-gatherers in Mainland South-East Asia", the Mlabri are an ethno-linguistic group living in scattered settlements across rural Northern Thailand. There is no good estimate on the population, though most sources land anywhere between 150-400 people. They blend traditional hunter-gathering and nomadic lifestyle strategies with working for other minoritiy ethnic groups in farms. I naively sought them out when I was a masters student, and have since routinely visited to live how they live, learn their language, and elicit data for research. Since then, it has become my favourite language spoken by my favourite people. 

Despite their kindness, the Mlabri are undergoing a humanitarian crisis. As an isolated group that has recently begun integration into `modern Thai society', there are issues of widespread poverty and social issues. If you are interested in supporting, Chris Davis and I routinely speak to the community and bring supplies funded via donation.
